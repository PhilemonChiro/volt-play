    Learn Volt by changing real code.

    Nine short courses, each on one idea. Labels point at the code that matters, you make the change, and the compiler checks it as you type.

    2. You make the change

      Each step asks for one small edit, right under the line it’s about. A hint is there if you want one.

    3. The compiler checks it

      The moment your change is right, the step confirms it and tells you why it mattered.
